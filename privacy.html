Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

What these tools do with your data

Every tool on this site runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you type, and any subtitle file you open, are processed on your own device. They are not sent to our servers and are not stored by us. We do not use cookies, and the pages do not save anything in your browser's storage.

What we collect

Nothing from your use of the tools. There is no sign-up, no account and no analytics or advertising code on these pages at the time of writing.

Hosting and logs

The pages are served by a third-party hosting service. Like any web host, it may keep standard server logs (for example your IP address, the page requested and the time) for security and operations. We do not control those logs; see the host's own privacy policy.

Links to other sites

Pages link to ORBIT Color's store on Gumroad. If you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Changes

If we later add analytics or advertising, we will update this page before doing so and describe what is collected.

Contact

Contact: please open an issue at github.com/aifukugyouacademy-hue/video-tools/issues.
