Recording Time & Storage Calculator

How long a card or drive will record at your bitrate, or how much space a shoot needs. Everything is calculated in your browser.

–

How it works

Data per second = (video bitrate + audio bitrate). Megabits per second divided by 8 gives megabytes per second. A 100 Mbps video with 1,536 kbps audio is about 12.7 MB per second, or about 45.6 GB per hour. Card sizes use decimal units (1 GB = 1,000 MB), as card makers do; a card also loses a little space to formatting, so keep a 5–10% margin.

Bitrates are averages. Variable-bitrate and RAW formats can run higher in busy scenes, so check your camera's real data rate and leave room.

Quick start

Enter the video and audio bitrates (or pick a preset). In the first tab, enter the card size to see how long it records. In the second tab, enter the hours and cameras to see the storage you need.
